AI-Powered Features
Intelligent tools to enhance your border crossing experience
📈

Predictive Queue Forecasting

Machine-learning models trained on 5+ years of historical traffic data provide accurate queue predictions up to 6 hours ahead for all checkpoints.

⚠

Anomaly & Incident Detection

Real-time AI monitoring flags unusual congestion spikes, incidents, or abnormal patterns and triggers automated alerts to operators and travellers.

🧭

Personalised Travel Recommendations

Based on your travel history and current conditions, the AI assistant suggests the optimal checkpoint, lane, and departure time for your journey.

🗣

AI Virtual Assistant (Chat)

A conversational AI chatbot answers traveller queries about immigration rules, required documents, and current wait times around the clock.

📷

Automated Lane Classification

Computer-vision AI analyses checkpoint camera feeds to automatically classify vehicle types and balance lane throughput in real time.

🌏

Cross-Border Event Intelligence

The system ingests public-holiday calendars, major events, and school-term data across Singapore and Malaysia to anticipate surge periods.
